Description
=======

The CobbleRegions: Kanto mod adds the 10 following locations from the 1st Generation Pokemon Games:
 - Pallet Town
 - Viridian City
 - Pewter City
 - Cerulean City
 - Vermilion City
 - Lavender Town
 - Celadon City
 - Saffron City
 - Fuchsia City
 - Cinnabar Island

Each town includes their unique buildings (i.e. gyms) and trainers that appeared in these towns in the games.

Gym leaders and other trainers reflect those from the Radical Cobblemon Trainers Mod's Radical Red Series. For information on trainer locations, check the 'Trainer Card' item or the Radical Red Nuzlocke Guide: https://nuzlocketracker.org/guides/radical-red

Featured Locations
============

8 of the featured locations include a gym structure. These gyms recreate their look from the FireRed/LeafGreen counterparts, including their unique puzzles.

For example, Sabrina's Gym (and the Silph Co. Building) include teleporter blocks that allow you to move from different rooms or floors in an instant!
